The Alliance History

Caleb's Studio & Chase McDillan (Clevis Corporation)

Great creations are rarely born in isolation. The strategic creative alliance between Caleb's Studio and Chase McDillan (Clevis Corporation) emerged from a shared passion for digital craftsmanship, precision configuration, and pushing the evolutionary boundaries of independent asset development.

Our collaborative journey originated as independent creators operating within the Richmond and Vancouver digital landscapes. By frequently cross-referencing, reviewing, and auditing each other's technical projects, intricate 3D assets, and complex logic structures, a profound synergy was formed. Over time, we recognized that by merging our technical insights, peer-reviewing our asset pipelines, and establishing a unified creative bond, we could deliver an uncompromised level of quality and innovation to the community.

Today, our platforms stand side-by-side, remaining completely autonomous in business operations and legal liabilities, yet deeply integrated in creative spirit, technical vision, and dedication to flawless asset engineering. We continuously advance our technological frontier by co-developing advanced products. This workflow leverages Caleb's Studio's specialized expertise to architecturalize the core Clevis Corporation phone systems alongside other high-tier consumer hardware produced under the Clevis banner.
